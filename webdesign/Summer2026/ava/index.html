Paik Kaung Sun: A Korean Immigrant Story

"Such strong, quiet courage in ordinary people in the face of danger is really something to admire and remember always."

In the early 1900s, the first wave of Korean immigrants began arriving in the United States in search of new opportunities while facing economic hardship, cultural barriers, and discrimination. Their journeys were shaped by the challenges of adapting to a new society while preserving their identities. The experiences of early Korean immigrants like Paik Kaung Sun reveal a complex history of resilience, struggle, and belonging within America.
