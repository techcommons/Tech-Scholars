Zitkála-Šá's Editorial Comments in the American Indian Magazine

In her editorial comments, Zitkála-Šá denounced government corruption. She criticized the Bureau of Indian Affairs for misusing tribal resources. She wanted the wartime contribution of Native American soldiers in World War I to argue they deserved full civic recognition. She argued that because Indigenous peoples were the original occupants of the land, they held an inherent right to vote and representation, mirroring American democratic ideals. This is a perfect example of how Zitkála-Šá used her writing to advocate for Indigenous rights.
