Zitkála-Šá (Red Bird): Activism and Cultural Preservation Through Art

"I seem to be in a spiritual unrest. I hate this eternal tug of war between being 'wild' or becoming 'civilized'. I am what I am. I owe no apologies to God or men."

Imagine using your voice to shape history. Zitkála-Šá, a Native American of the Yankton Dakota Sioux writer, musician, educator, and activist dedicated her life and worked tirelessly to preserve Indigenous culture and promote the rights of Native Americans. Through her powerful stories and advocacy, she left a lasting legacy that still inspires people today.

Gertrude Käsebier, "Zitkála-Šá in 1898", 1898. World History Encyclopedia, July 23, 2026
