Housewives in The Great Depression

Women's roles in budgeting and helping their families survive during economic challenges in American History

"The women know that life must go on and that the needs of life must be met and it is their courage and determination which, time and again, have pulled us through worse crises than the present one." -Eleanor Roosevelt

The Great Depression (1929-1939) changed the lives of millions of Americans, but women played a pivotal role in helping families survive. Through careful budgeting, meal planning, and resilience, housewives stretched their limited resources in order to provide food and stability for their families during one of the nation's toughest economic catastrophes.
