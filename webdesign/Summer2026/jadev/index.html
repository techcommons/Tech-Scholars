The Movement

Click Here for Zines

The punk movement is a form of counterculture: anti-establishment, opposed to large exploitative corporations, rigid hierarchies, and other 'mainstream' ideologies that create inequality. Punks are an alternative group of people that are considered loud about their views against social injustice and for self sustainability. Punk goes beyond music, its an ideology which has been expressed through various forms of media like fashion, literature, and other visual arts. Unfortunately the punk scene especially in earlier years was male dominated; some were skeptical, or believed women weren't cut out to participate in a hardcore movement. Many women felt overshadowed, so feminist activists got to work. What emerged was their own subculture, known as riot grrrl, where women are encouraged to participate in punk culture.
