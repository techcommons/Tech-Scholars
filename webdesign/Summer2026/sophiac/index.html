Soilders of the Civil War

The stories of women and gender non conforming soldiers who fought in the Civil War.

Soldiers are often thought of as almost larger than life public heroes, but what happens when their private lives are not what you expect. During the Civil War there were over 400 documented accounts of people assigned female at birth disguising themselves as men to fight on both sides of the war. For much of US history, women have been relegated to private life, and it was less acceptable for women to express themselves in a strong heroic way like men do. Men get to fight for the "glory of the nation" in public while women's labor of managing the home life while men are gone at war is often forgotten. So what happens when ambitious and patriotic women push themselves into the public eye and fight for what they believe in and what were the public and private gender expressions of soldiers like during the civil war?
