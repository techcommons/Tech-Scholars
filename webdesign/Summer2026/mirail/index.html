The Fate of Jeane Gardiner: A Tragic Coincidence or a Tale of Justice

Mirai Iwai-Louie

Uncredited. The Bermudian, Accessed July 24, 2026.

In order to find a scapegoat to blame for plagues, epidemics, failures in crops, and other misfortunes, witchcraft accusations and trials became quite popular around the 17th century. This website allows you to explore the case of Jeane Gardiner, one of the five executed "witches" in Bermuda, and unpack the social implications that led to such accusations.
