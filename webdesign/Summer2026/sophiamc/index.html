Activism Through Literature in the Fight for Racial Equality

Introduction

For years, in order to speak up for racial equality, women used literature, and writing to fight and speak up against racial inequality. Women such as Phillis Wheatley and Angelina Weld Grimké both used their talented writing skills to fight for what they believed was right, and against the injustice they had experienced as African American women growing up, and living in an white-male dominated America.
