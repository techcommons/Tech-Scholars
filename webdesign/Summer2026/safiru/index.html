A Marriage Before Its Time: Charity Bryant and Sylvia Drake
  • "Send, Gracious Heavens! Thy best of blessings send,
  • Ye Guardian Powers! Protect my lovely friend.
  • Let grief within her bosom find no place
  • Visit her soul with thy peculiar grace
  • In all her steps, may she thy goodness prove
  • Attune her heart to Gratitude and Love.
  • Divest her mind of every needless fear,
  • Restore her health, and make her still thy care,
  • And O! to Thou! (as 'tis to Thee alone
  • Kind Parent, that her grips, and wants ever known)
  • Espouse her cause, accept her as Thine own."
  • -Charity Bryant
