Anna Maria Lane

Info of Anna Maria Lane (1755-1810) is a mysterious woman with little to no known early history. In her life, she accomplished many a great thing. From surviving a potentialy fatal bullet wound, to helping many American soldiers survive throughout many famous battles.Commonly, women would opt to be camp followers, who tended to the injured soldiers and made food for the army. However, Lane decided to join the fighting using the identity of a man, which was super dangerous for a woman to do, not only because of the dangers of war, but because if she got caught, she could face extreme repurcusions. After being wounded in battle, Anna Maria Lane continued to support the American cause and was recognized for her courage. Because of the injuries she received while serving, the government later awarded her a yearly pension to help support her. This made her one of the first women in the United States to receive financial recognition for military service. Although many details about her life have been lost over time, Anna Maria Lane is remembered today for her bravery, determination, and willingness to risk everything for her country.
